20 November 2026 in any time zone". So a story first reported overnight 19→20 Nov counts as
Friday's news when its first report lands on 20 Nov in Asian timezones (WIB/IST/JST) — that is
the Friday morning news cycle: the Shenzhen declaration (Independent 00:01 UTC 20 Nov; Reuters
wrapup 02:41 IST), the Wall Street close (AP 21:16 UTC 19 Nov = 04:16 WIB 20 Nov), the Berlin
talks (19:43 UTC 19 Nov = 02:43 WIB 20 Nov), GTA VI's $1.84bn tally (17:06 UTC 19 Nov = 00:06
WIB 20 Nov), the Pete Rose pardon (23:57 UTC 19 Nov = 06:57 WIB 20 Nov), the Senate war-powers
vote (Thursday evening US = Friday WIB). Anything first reported Thursday in Asia too is OUT
of the featured set and goes in the DATE CHECK box.

## LEADS (3, equal — never one hero 60%+)

### 1 — SHENZHEN · DIPLOMACY — "Trump, Xi and Putin issue a joint peace declaration"
- The first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral (Thu 19 Nov, Kylin Villa, Shenzhen, ~90 min, Xi
  chairing) ends in a four-point "China-Russia-U.S. Heads of State Declaration toward Peace",
  published overnight: (1) reform the UN; (2) increase production/supply of refined petroleum
  products; (3) the ICC "must not abuse their jurisdiction" (Putin's 2023 warrant stands);
  (4) "a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought" + a dialogue on reducing nuclear
  risks and proliferation — no date, no warhead cuts, no arms-control talks.
- It mentions NEITHER Ukraine NOR Iran; Putin admitted it "did not set ambitious goals" and
  nobody would be forced to comply. Trump holds a news conference in Washington at noon
  Friday (17:00 UTC). White House + Xinhua + Kremlin published the text; the White House
  published no separate US readout.
- Sources: Reuters wrapup via Devdiscourse (published 2026-11-20T02:41 IST), The Independent
  (2026-11-20T00:01:43Z), The Times, Bernama via PJM (20 Nov 05:55 WIB).

### 2 — ODISHA · STORM — "Cyclone Akvan makes landfall at noon"
- Severe Cyclonic Storm Akvan crosses the coast near Gopalpur (between Narsapur and Puri) by
  noon Friday 20 Nov (06:30 UTC), winds 90–100 km/h gusting 110, 20–30 cm of rain; 3rd Bay of
  Bengal cyclone in under a month (after Arnab and Murasu).
- "Three lakh" (300,000) evacuated; 423 rescue teams deployed; 69 trains cancelled; red
  alerts in 11 districts; 80 fishermen stranded at sea sheltered at Aryapalli jetty; Odisha
  aims to evacuate 200,000 in total, target zero casualties. Counts rose through Thu:
  ~47,000 → 1,05,304 by Thu afternoon (Chief Secretary Anu Garg) → 300,000+ by Friday
  (Jagran live tracker). Use 300,000 — the Friday figure.
- Sources: IMD via The Hindu/Jagran live tracker (20 Nov), ZenithMap, NewsXphere.

### 3 — WASHINGTON · IRAN — "Trump puts the Iran decision off to 2027"
- Trump says he will "see what happens" after January 1 and that there is no rush on a
  decision on Iran — pushing the war decision into 2027. Reported 20 Nov 03:06–03:13 IST
  (Reuters + TOI). Context: the US–Israel war on Iran started 28 Feb (Day 266); the Senate
  on Thursday rejected a war-powers resolution curbing Trump's Iran war powers 51–48
  (only Collins, Murkowski, Paul broke ranks) — first Senate Iran vote since the 3 Nov
  midterms. Netanyahu presses for new strikes.
- Sources: Reuters (20 Nov 03:06), Times of India (20 Nov 03:13 IST), Reuters Senate vote
  (2026-11-19).

### 5 — ANTALYA · CLIMATE — "COP31's closing day: fossil fuels cut from the draft"
- COP31 enters its scheduled closing day Friday 20 Nov with no final deal: a slimmed-down
  draft published Thursday cut 144 paragraphs to 80, removed EVERY reference to fossil
  fuels (no "fossil", coal, oil or gas) and dropped the $120bn adaptation-finance figure —
  only a call for further talks toward tripling adaptation finance by 2035 remains. The
  draft keeps the 35% electrification-by-2035 goal. Final text due by midnight in Antalya;
  recent COPs regularly run 24h+ over. Chris Bowen (Australia) leads the negotiations.
- Sources: Asia Today (published 20 Nov 06:41 WIB), Kaer News, Watan News (COP31 closes
  Fri 20 Nov).

### 6 — BERLIN · DIPLOMACY — "Europe ready for Ukraine talks with Russia"

  war — but only with Europe and Kyiv at the table, and only after a Ukraine–Russia peace
  deal. Merz: Moscow's "maximalist demands are not realistic". UK PM Andy Burnham: Europe
  has "a clear and consistent and united negotiating position". Kushner (Trump envoy):
  Trump wants a deal "with Europe by his side". Putin has so far rejected a European role;
  after Shenzhen he said the US was "insistently" asking for Europe to take part.
- Sources: BBC via The Brief (19 Nov 19:43 UTC), Brussels Reporter, Hillingdon Times
